Add CareHub to your phone

Open the shared barn from an icon on your home screen.

Scan with your phone

Point your phone’s camera at the square and tap the link that appears.

Install on this phone

iPhone or iPad

  1. In Safari, open the CareHub home page.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an up arrow), then Add to Home Screen. Share may be under the More menu.
  3. If offered, turn on Open as Web App. Tap Add.

Android phone or tablet

  1. In Chrome, open the CareHub home page.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install and create shortcut.
  3. Choose Install if offered, or Create shortcut and then Add.

Use your own CareHub login. Connect to the internet to save barn records.
